ESP32 Cat Eyes — 360×360 PNG assets

Black background, white eyes only, with a subtle cool glow (shadowBlur) around the white elements for a robotic look. Each Download button saves a real 360×360 PNG file. The sleep state (idle_sleep_f0–f3) is a continuous 4-frame loop at ~5 fps. The idle glance frames (idle_f0–f3) are NOT a loop: pupils rest centered on idle_f0 most of the time; every few seconds play 2–3 frames slowly toward one side, then drift back to idle_f0.
